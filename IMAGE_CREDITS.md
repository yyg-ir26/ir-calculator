# Anatomy Image Credits and Licenses

The following third-party anatomy images are included in the IR Decision Support Suite. The image files are distributed as separate assets in `assets/anatomy/` and have not been edited for this app. The app displays them with responsive sizing only.

Please retain this file and the individual source/license information when copying or redistributing the app. Each image remains subject to its own license. Creative Commons BY licenses require appropriate credit, a link to the license, and an indication of changes if any are made. Creative Commons BY-SA licenses additionally require adaptations of the licensed image to be shared under the same or a compatible license. The BY-SA images listed below are included unchanged.
